1.1 Introducing Calculus: Can Change Occur at an Instant?

Average Rate of Change
Interactive contentTap or hover over highlighted terms for vocabulary, the icons for key formulas, and any line, point or curve on a graph to learn more about it.

In Unit 1.1 of AP Calculus AB/BC, you start by thinking about how a quantity changes over an interval. The average rate of changeVocabularyHow much a function's output changes per unit of input between two points: the slope of the secant line through them. tells you how much a function changes between two inputs. It is calculated using:

f(b) − f(a)b − a Key formulaAverage rate of change of f on [a, b]: change in output divided by change in input.

This represents the slope of the line connecting two points on the graph.
